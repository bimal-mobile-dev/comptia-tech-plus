# [Domain-2](#domain-2-infrastructure) **Infrastructure**

- **24% weighting — the single heaviest domain on the exam.** Covers hardware and networking fundamentals, the most content-dense area of Tech+

## Computer hardware

- **Core components**: motherboard, CPU, RAM, storage drives (HDD vs. SSD), and power supply — recognizing each component's basic function and how they connect
- **Peripherals**: input devices (keyboard, mouse), output devices (monitor, printer), and common connector/port types (USB, HDMI, etc.)

## Networking fundamentals

- **Network types**: LAN (Local Area Network), WAN (Wide Area Network), and the devices that connect them (routers, switches, access points)
- **IP addressing basics**: recognizing the general purpose of an IP address and the difference between a public and private address, at an introductory level
- **Wired vs. wireless connectivity**: understanding the trade-offs (speed, reliability, convenience) between Ethernet and Wi-Fi connections

## Internet service and connectivity types

- **ISP connection types**: DSL, cable, fiber, and satellite — recognizing their general speed and reliability characteristics
- **Cloud vs. on-premises infrastructure**: a conceptual introduction to the difference between hosting infrastructure locally vs. through a cloud provider, setting up the deeper IaaS/PaaS/SaaS treatment in Domain 3
